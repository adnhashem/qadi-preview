Product core · for Abdullah

He did not ask for an archive. He asked for a method that works with him.

The judge replied to our staff-portal prototype with “not there yet” and sent a link to a site he built for another domain. That site tells us exactly what he values. This brief explains the gap, proposes a different core for the project, and ends with two questions only the judge can answer.

For: AbdullahPrepared by: Adnan · TheTeamStatus: proposal — pending the judge’s answers

1His site is a working method, not a document store

The link he sent (Investigating Medical Errors and Professional Violations, by Dr. Mishal Al-Zaabi, Medical Liability Authority) is a practical guide for investigators, turned into an interactive page. It has no database and no login. What it has is:

  • A seven-step track you tick off for a specific case — receive the assignment, check impartiality, build the file, hear the parties, analyse, write the result, review and notify.
  • A deadline calculator for the statutory time limits.
  • Fillable templates: case plan, evidence index, hearing preparation sheet, report components, pre-submission checklist.
  • Reasoning templates: “if causation is proven… / if only the violation is proven… / if nothing is proven…”.
  • A worked example, a self-test, and every paragraph tagged by its weight (legal rule · regulatory procedure · judicial application · suggested tool).
What this tells us

His mental model is “a tool that walks with me while I work” — steps, forms, checks, deadlines. We sent him “a place to store and search files”. That is the whole gap behind “not there yet”.

2The difference we can offer: the same method, but with memory

His site is written once for a whole profession and remembers nothing about the person using it (progress lives in the browser; templates are copied out as text). We can keep the shape he already likes and change the heart:

DimensionHis site (static method)Proposed core (living method)
The trackSeven generic steps for any caseThe ruling path for each appeal: admissibility → list the grounds → match each ground against settled principles → reasons → disposition → consistency check → sign-off
MemoryNoneHis own rulings feed every step: “as settled in this court’s jurisprudence” is pulled from his archive, with the citation
TemplatesFill in, copy as textThe ruling is drafted inside the tool, section by section — so indexing becomes a by-product, not extra work
ChecksA manual checklistConsistency against his previous rulings + drafting checks (our sample already showed impossible dates and may/may-not slips) + deadlines on the case itself

In short: “indexing” — the word Abdullah used from day one — does not sell as a search screen. It sells when it happens by itself because the judge rules inside the tool. And what makes it his, not generic: the tool knows his principles, his fixed phrasing, his recurring errors.

3Honest caveat: this is an inference, not a confirmed requirement

We have three signals, all indirect:

  • The recordings: indexing + analysis + search + MCP.
  • His site: he values a method as steps and tools, not empty pages.
  • “Not there yet”: what he saw was below his expectation — but it does not say what the expectation is.
The risk

He may want exactly his own site, re-done for cassation rulings — a methodical guide plus rulings indexed by principle — without any of the “living path” above. If so, our proposal is bigger than his request. unverified · single source

That is why we should not build on a guess. One sentence from the judge saves us a week.

4Two questions for the judge

  1. “When you open the system in the morning, what is the first thing you want to do in it?”Tells us whether the core is a case he is working on (the path), a ruling he is looking for (the archive), or a principle he wants to verify (consistency). Everything else follows from this answer.
  2. “The medical-errors site — what in it would you want for yourself here, and what is it missing?”He built it, so he knows its limits. His answer separates what he likes about the form (steps, templates, checklist) from what he misses in the substance (memory, his own rulings, checks).
Next step

Abdullah puts the two questions to the judge. With the answers, we rebuild the prototype around the confirmed core — on the same link, in the same design — within days, not weeks.
